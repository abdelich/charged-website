FAQ

The questions clients ask first

How quickly can you start?

Usually within 2–5 business days of an agreement, and faster for urgent projects.

How big is your team?

A small permanent core of developers, backed by a trusted network of specialists we bring in per project. Everyone has higher education, certifications, and real delivery experience — and the team scales to the work rather than the other way around.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope and complexity. We work on either a fixed price or an hourly rate, with better rates for long-term clients. You get a budget range before committing to anything.

Do you guarantee quality?

Yes. We use proven technologies, test thoroughly, and provide a warranty period on delivered work. SLA-based support is available if you need it.

Do you work under NDA?

Yes, signed on request. A good share of our portfolio is confidential work for private businesses.

What happens after launch?

You get handover and documentation, plus optional ongoing maintenance and further development under a separate agreement.

Is Charged the same company as Charged Development?

Yes. Charged Development is our full name, and people shorten it in all sorts of ways — Charged, Charged Tech, Charged Sweden. It is one team, based in Stockholm, Sweden, with a single website: chargeddata.com.

Which countries do you work in?

The company is registered in Sweden and operates fully remotely. We have delivered projects across Europe, North America, Ukraine, and elsewhere — there are no geographical restrictions.
